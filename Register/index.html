Zero Cloud Dependency

Cryptographic Security by Design

Your license belongs to you. No DRM servers to contact, no periodic activation checks, and zero telemetry.

Ed25519 Asymmetric Signatures

Every license is signed using modern RFC 8032 Ed25519 elliptic-curve cryptography. The Smaran desktop client verifies the mathematical signature locally using its embedded public key.

Steganographic LSB Encoding

The entire cryptographic envelope is embedded directly into the least significant bits of the license card image pixels. A single PNG file contains your human-readable badge and machine-readable certificate.

100% Offline & Private

Smaran never connects to any server to validate or renew your license. You can use your software on fully air-gapped machines, workstations without internet, or secure studio networks.

Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import this license card into Smaran?

Download your generated .png card, open Smaran on your PC, click Settings, go to the License tab, and click Import License. Choose the downloaded image and your services activate instantly.

Can I use one license across multiple personal PCs?

Yes. Your license is cryptographically verified locally on any machine where Smaran is installed. You can store your license card image on a flash drive and import it on both your desktop and laptop.

What happens if a service is turned off in my license?

Services not granted in your license display a subtle locked indicator in their respective tabs. All licensed features continue to operate with full functionality and zero restrictions.

How does Lifetime validity work?

Selecting 100 Years creates a permanent lifetime license. It will remain valid through 2126 with no recurring renewals or expiration popups.
