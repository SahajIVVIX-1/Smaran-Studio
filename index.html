Smaran

Gentle reminders, on your schedule.

Type what to remember and when, the way you’d say it. At the right moment Smaran slides in a small, calm card: press Done, or Snooze for later. It lives quietly in the Windows tray, and now it can run your PC’s routines too.

Features

Small on screen. Thoughtful underneath.

Everything a reminder needs, and nothing that gets in your way.

Any schedule

Once, every few minutes or hours, daily at several times, weekly on chosen days, monthly (even “the last day”) or yearly. Repeating reminders can keep to set hours and weekdays.

Cards that slide in

A small pill with the title that opens into the full card when you point at it, with a soft colour light running round its border. Swipe it away, or right-click for more.

Normal or important

Normal reminders hide themselves after a few seconds. Important ones stay until you act and come through quiet hours and full-screen apps.

Quiet hours and focus

Set quiet hours, and Smaran holds normal reminders while a full-screen app, game or presentation is showing, then delivers them afterwards. Pause everything from the tray.

Rotating lines

Give a reminder a list of lines (stretches, affirmations, tips) and it shows the next one each time, in order or shuffled.

Snooze and follow-ups

Snooze for 5 minutes, 15 minutes, an hour or until tomorrow. If you don’t respond, Smaran can remind you again every few minutes, up to the number of times you choose.

B

Bold, italic, underline

Style words in a title or note with B, I, U and S (or Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I, Ctrl+U). The style shows on the card and in your lists.

The Smaran dots

The logo is alive: three dots dance and form shapes in the title bar, the tray and on each card. They ring for a reminder, tick on Done and sleep while paused.

History

What happened to each reminder (done, snoozed, missed, skipped) and every automation run, with a weekly summary. Missed while the PC was off? Smaran tells you once, gently.

Your look

Light, dark or follow Windows. Pick the corner and screen, three card sizes, a chime per reminder, your own fonts, and a colour for each reminder.

Backups built in

Every change keeps rolling backups. Back up to a file, restore it, or add reminders from another file, including older Smaran versions.

Stays in the tray

Starts quietly when you sign in. Closing the window keeps reminders running; the tray shows what’s next and lets you pause or show one now.

New in 2.14

Automations. Your PC’s routines, done for you.

Like a friendly Task Scheduler: pick when, pick what, and Smaran does it. Chain up to 20 steps, with a wait before any of them.

When

  • At a date and time, or on a repeat (once, every N minutes, daily, weekly, monthly, yearly)
  • When the computer starts, when you sign in, or when Smaran opens (with an optional wait)
  • When you lock or unlock the PC
  • When it goes to sleep or wakes up
  • When the charger is plugged in or unplugged
  • When you’ve been away for some minutes, or come back

Do

  • Lock, sign out, sleep, hibernate, shut down or restart
  • Open any app, with arguments if you like
  • Open any file: a PDF, a document, a folder
  • Run a script (.bat, .cmd or .ps1, with or without a window)
  • Open a link in your default browser, or the one you choose (Chrome, Edge, Firefox… found for you, with a private-window option)

Safe by default

  • Before signing out, sleeping or shutting down, a card counts down 30 seconds with Cancel and Run Now
  • A time missed while the PC was asleep or off is skipped, never run late
  • Every run is in History: ran, failed (with the reason), cancelled or skipped
Bedtime
Daily at 23:30 → Shut down

The safety card. Press Cancel and nothing happens.

How it works

Three steps, then it just runs.

Install

Run Smaran Setup. It installs straight away and opens Smaran when it’s done. Or use the portable exe from any folder.

Add what matters

Type a reminder, or press Ctrl+N. Two gentle examples are ready for you to edit or delete.

Get on with your day

Close the window. Smaran waits in the tray and brings each reminder to you at the right time.
